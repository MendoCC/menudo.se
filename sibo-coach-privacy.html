Privacy Policy — SIBO Coach

Last updated: 20 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Menudo Consulting AB ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the SIBO Coach mobile application (the "App") for Android and iOS. SIBO Coach helps people living with SIBO (Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth) track meals, symptoms, and supplements, and offers an optional AI Coach.

The short version: Your health data lives on your device. We do not require an account, we do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising. When you choose to use the AI Coach, only the context needed to answer your question is sent to our secure processing service.

1. Who we are

Menudo Consulting AB, Org.nr 556896-1758, Estrids väg 1, 187 72 Täby, Sweden. Contact: ekonomi@menudo.se.

2. Data stored on your device

The information you enter into the App — including meals, symptoms, supplements, notes, and related timestamps — is stored locally on your device in the App's private database. This data is not uploaded to us or automatically synced to any server. If you delete the App, this local data is removed with it.

3. The AI Coach

The AI Coach is an optional feature. When you send a message to the AI Coach, the App sends the following to our processing service in order to generate a response:

  • the message you type;
  • a limited summary of relevant health context (for example recent symptoms or foods) needed to make the answer useful;
  • an anonymous, randomly generated installation identifier and a language/locale hint.

Our processing service is a secure backend operated by us on Cloudflare's infrastructure. It forwards the request to our AI model provider, Anthropic, which generates the reply and returns it to you. We do not attach your name, email, or account to these requests, because the App does not require one.

The AI Coach provides general educational information only. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Always seek the advice of your physician regarding a medical condition.

4. The anonymous installation identifier

To operate the AI Coach fairly (for example to apply trial limits and usage quotas and to prevent abuse), the App generates a random identifier for your installation the first time you use the AI Coach. This identifier is not derived from your device hardware, phone number, email, or any personal detail, and it cannot be used to identify you. It is reset if you reinstall the App.

5. Subscriptions and payments

SIBO Coach offers an optional paid subscription (SIBO Coach Pro). Purchases and renewals are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms and privacy policies. We never receive or store your payment card details. We receive only the subscription status needed to unlock Pro features.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or build advertising profiles.
  • We do not require you to create an account or provide your name or email to use the App.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

7. Third-party processors

We rely on a small number of service providers who process data only on our behalf to deliver the App's features:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosts our secure backend that handles AI Coach requests.
  • Anthropic, PBC — provides the AI model that generates AI Coach responses.
  • Apple and Google — distribute the App and process subscription payments.

8. Data retention

Your tracking data is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. AI Coach requests are processed to generate a response and to enforce usage limits; we do not maintain a personal profile linked to you from these requests.

9. Children

SIBO Coach is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights

Because your health data is stored locally, you are in direct control of it and can view, edit, or delete it within the App at any time. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, you may have rights to access, correct, or erase personal data we process, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights or ask a question, contact us at ekonomi@menudo.se.

11. Security

Requests between the App and our backend are transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email ekonomi@menudo.se or write to Menudo Consulting AB, Estrids väg 1, 187 72 Täby, Sweden.

SIBO Coach is a product of Menudo Consulting AB. This policy applies specifically to the SIBO Coach mobile application.
