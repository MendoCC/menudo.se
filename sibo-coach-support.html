Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Your meals, symptoms, and supplements are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us or synced to a server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How does the “Find your triggers” analysis work?

When you log meals and symptoms, SIBO Coach looks at your whole history and tests which foods statistically line up with your symptoms. Each result shows how often a symptom followed a food, a risk ratio with a 95% confidence interval, and a significance value. It only shows a pattern when the data actually supports it, and it groups related foods (for example garlic, onion, and wheat as “fructans”) to reveal patterns that single foods can’t. All of this runs entirely on your device — your log never leaves your phone for this analysis.

These are statistical associations in your own log, not proof that a food causes a symptom, and not medical advice. Use them as a starting point to discuss with a professional.

Can I share a report with my dietitian or doctor?

Yes. In the Correlations section (Pro), tap Export report to create a clean one-page PDF of your food–symptom associations, including the date range and the method used. You can then share it by email, save it to Files, or print it to bring to an appointment.

How does the AI Coach work?

When you send a message to the AI Coach, the app sends your message and a limited summary of your recent health context to our secure backend, which generates a response. No account, name, or email is attached — only an anonymous per-install identifier.

What is SIBO Coach Pro?

Pro is an optional subscription ($9.99/month) that unlocks the app’s analysis: the food–symptom correlation engine, FODMAP-group insights, the practitioner PDF export, dashboard trends, and the AI Coach. The free tier always includes unlimited tracking, your full history, and the timeline. A 7-day free trial of the AI Coach is available for new subscribers.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play, not by us.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SIBO Coach → Cancel.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → SIBO Coach → Cancel.
How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the app, go to the upgrade screen, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same App Store or Google Play account you used to subscribe.

How do I delete my data?

You can edit or delete any entry directly in the app. To remove everything, delete the app from your device — because your data is stored locally, uninstalling removes it.

Is SIBO Coach medical advice?

No. SIBO Coach is a wellness and lifestyle tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making changes to your diet, supplements, or treatment.
